# TaskLink PH
A milestone escrow ledger removing high-margin platforms to optimize cross-border payments for Filipino freelancers.

### Problem & Solution
Juan is a freelance graphic designer in Cebu, Philippines, who relies on platforms like Upwork to find overseas clients but loses over 15% of his earnings to platform take-rates, structural transaction margins, and slow multi-day bank drawdowns that delay his ability to pay basic utility bills. TaskLink PH resolves this by building a light, on-chain task tracking architecture where clients lock payment assets directly into Soroban smart contracts, bypassing fees and unlocking immediate local off-ramp liquidity.
